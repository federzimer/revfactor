# The RevFactor Method

RevFactor manages STR revenue in three phases:

1. **Discover.** Understand the property, market, comp set, and the host's goals; establish baseline pacing and yield.
2. **Strategise.** Build the pricing strategy — base rates, seasonality, minimum stays, lead-time curves, and gap-filling rules — calibrated to live market data.
3. **Optimise.** Monitor daily, adjust to demand signals, and refine over time.

This is strategy plus hands-on calibration, not a set-and-forget algorithm. See [dynamic pricing for STR](/okf/dynamic-pricing.md).
