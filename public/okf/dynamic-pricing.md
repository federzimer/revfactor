# Dynamic Pricing for Short-Term Rentals

Dynamic pricing adjusts nightly rates based on demand, seasonality, lead time, day of week, events, and competitive supply. Tools like PriceLabs automate the mechanics, but the strategy behind the settings is what drives results: base prices, min-stay rules, lead-time curves, and how aggressively to fill gaps.

RevFactor combines tools like PriceLabs with hands-on calibration so pricing reflects each market's real demand rather than generic defaults. Hosts keep control; RevFactor supplies the strategy and daily oversight. Related: [ADR vs RevPAR](/okf/adr-vs-revpar.md).
