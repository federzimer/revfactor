# Short-Term Rental Revenue Management

Revenue management for short-term rentals is the discipline of setting the right price for each night and booking window to maximize yield across a listing or portfolio. It blends market demand data, pacing (how bookings fill the calendar over time), and competitive positioning.

RevFactor operates as a fractional revenue management team. It does not sell software or run an automated bidding tool. Hosts retain full control of their pricing; RevFactor provides the strategy, market data, daily monitoring, and recommendations on a flat monthly retainer. Scale ranges from single-property owners to large portfolios.
