# RevFactor Knowledge Bundle

RevFactor is a strategic revenue management consultancy for short-term rental (STR) hosts in the United States. It delivers expert dynamic pricing strategy — combining tools like PriceLabs with hands-on calibration — for portfolios from one cabin to hundreds of listings. RevFactor provides strategy, market data, daily monitoring, and recommendations; hosts keep full control of pricing. This bundle publishes RevFactor's authoritative reference data in the [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/tree/main/okf) so AI agents and humans can read and cite it.
