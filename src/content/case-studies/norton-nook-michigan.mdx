## The on-track-to-match story

This property's headline isn't the +189% pacing lift — it's how close the calendar already sits to last year's full-summer revenue.

Final Jun–Aug 2025 revenue was **$36,030.** As of the May 2026 data pull, OTB pacing for the same window is **$35,802 — within 0.6% of last year's complete number, with peak weeks still in the booking window.** That's not a lift story; that's a _matching-while-leading-the-market_ story.

The Market Penetration Index sits at **1.29×** — solidly market-leading — and **August specifically is pacing at 1.55× MPI on $10,365 booked revenue, vs. just $1,313 same-time-last-year.** Late summer in a Lake Michigan submarket typically softens. This calendar is doing the opposite.

## The multi-property operator angle

Thea Cabanilla operates this property under **Topaz Stays LLC** — a multi-property STR business that also operates RevFactor-managed listings in other markets (including the Bourbon Trail Hot Tub property in Frankfort, KY). That cross-market visibility is part of what makes RevFactor's playbook compound for portfolio operators: pricing learnings from one submarket inform positioning in another, and the same revenue-management cadence runs across all properties in the portfolio.

This case study isn't just "what works for a single Lake Michigan listing" — it's "what works when the same operator wants the same discipline across a small portfolio."

## What changed — Fede's read

> *Norton Shores is a Lake Michigan submarket with two challenges: peak compresses into a narrow window, and the comp-set restrictions tighten right when demand is supposed to peak. The properties that get hurt the most in late summer are the ones running rigid five- and seven-night minimums into August — at that point, the leisure-travel calendar is fragmented, the back-to-school week is approaching, and a five-night minimum is filtering you out of half the searches.*
>
> *The work here was three things. We took the rate up two cycles in a row — $494 final 2024, $590 STLY, $689 OTB. That's not a single price increase, that's a comp-set move. We held bookings at premium early in the curve — 56.5% on-the-books at the data pull means we weren't waiting for last-minute discounted bookings to fill. And we opened minimum-stay rules into August where the comp-set was over-restricting — that's the 1.55× MPI in the market's softest month with 51.6% OTB occupancy against just 6.5% same-time-last-year.*
>
> *The portfolio context matters here too. Topaz Stays runs multiple properties under our management, including Bourbon Trail Hot Tub in Frankfort, Kentucky. The same pacing discipline runs across the portfolio, and the cross-market reads inform each individual decision.*
