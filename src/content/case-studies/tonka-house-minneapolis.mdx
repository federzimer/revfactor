## The two-cycle repositioning

Most pricing wins look like a single inflection point. This one is two.

The property's average daily rate has climbed from **$277 in 2025 final → $342 same-time-last-year → $465 on-the-books for summer 2026** — a **+68% lift over 24 months**, layered on top of a **+101.3% summer pacing lift** vs. STLY. The Market Penetration Index sits at **1.29×** for the summer in aggregate and **1.89×** in August specifically — meaning when the broader Minneapolis-metro market softens into late summer, this property doesn't.

This is what slow-and-steady positioning looks like when the math compounds. Each cycle, the rate moves up; each cycle, the comp-set moves to support it; each cycle, the calendar fills earlier in the booking window.

## The Minneapolis-metro context

Hopkins sits inside RevFactor's **5-property Minneapolis-metro concentration** — including this owner's second property, **Hutton House** (a 3BR Minneapolis listing also under RevFactor management, pacing **+152.9% in Q1 2026**). That dual-property visibility lets the team triangulate market behavior across two property types in the same submarket.

The market itself is a steady-state environment — 33.8% summer market occupancy, no extreme peaks, no extreme troughs. That's the kind of market where revenue management _quietly_ wins: not by riding a peak, but by capturing 30–50% more share in months when most operators are coasting.

## What changed — Fede's read

> *Minneapolis-metro is a steady-state market. There's no peak weekend that doubles your year. There's no Gatlinburg-style event compression. What you have is a 33% market occupancy band that holds across the summer, and the operators who win in markets like this are the ones doing the work quietly every week.*
>
> *Tonka House is two cycles of rate-floor work compounded. $277 final 2024, $342 STLY, $465 OTB. Sixty-eight percent ADR lift in 24 months is what happens when you move the comp-set position one step at a time, let occupancy stabilize at the new rate, then move it again. We're not chasing a peak. We're moving the floor.*
>
> *August's 1.89× MPI is the cleanest signal. When the market softens late summer, properties that ran rigid minimum-stay rules and rigid rates lose share. We're capturing roughly twice our expected share in the softest month — that's length-of-stay-discount work and minimum-stay flexibility, layered on a rate position the rest of the market hasn't matched yet.*
>
> *Same owner runs Hutton House over in Minneapolis proper — a 3BR pacing +152.9% in Q1. Same playbook, two different submarkets, single strategist watching both calendars. The portfolio effect makes the next decision easier than the last.*
