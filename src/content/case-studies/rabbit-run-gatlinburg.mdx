## The fast-time-to-value story

This is the case study that answers the question RevFactor gets most on intake calls: _how fast does this actually work?_

The property onboarded **February 9, 2026.** By the data pull on April 30, **2.8 months** of tenure later, summer pacing had moved from $21,501 same-time-last-year to **$68,960 on-the-books — a +$47,459 swing.** July alone is pacing $34,685 with a 1.70× Market Penetration Index against a 45.7% market occupancy.

That's not eight months of optimization. That's eight weeks.

## What the headline number obscures

The +220.7% pacing lift looks dramatic in part because the prior year's same-period booking curve was unusually thin ($21,501 STLY). The cleaner read is **vs. final 2025 revenue** for the same window: $70,780 final last year, $68,960 already booked this year — pacing **2.6% under final LY with peak July still booking.** July finished 2025 at a strong number; July 2026 is already at $34,685 OTB with a 77% occupancy committed.

In other words: this property had a strong 2025. It is on track to match or exceed it, with two-thirds of the peak month already locked in at a higher rate.

## The Smoky Mountains market context

Gatlinburg sits inside RevFactor's largest concentration of properties — **31 listings across the Smoky Mountains region.** That density gives the team an unusually clean comp-set read: when this property's pacing curve diverges from the market, it's visible immediately, and the playbook for what's working in the market in real time is informed by 30 sister properties.

The market is also unforgiving. Average daily rates in Gatlinburg sit at roughly $1,000–$1,400 for premium cabins. A wrong rate decision on a peak weekend costs four figures, not three.

## What changed in the first 60 days

The visible pattern in the calendar — without disclosing owner-specific configuration:

1. **ADR re-baselined.** OTB ADR is $1,352 vs. $1,265 same-time-last-year (+6.9%). The 2025 final ADR for the same window was $1,041 — meaning the property is now pacing **+30% over 2024 actuals**, layered on top of an already-higher 2025 number.
2. **Minimum-stay rules re-cut.** The 1.06× June MPI is roughly market-paced, but July and August jump to 1.70× and 1.25× — consistent with a minimum-stay structure that loosens into peak weeks where the market over-restricts.
3. **Pacing-led calendar.** The 77.4% July occupancy 8 weeks out from the booking window is the kind of pre-peak commitment that requires discipline early — holding rate when the comp set begins discounting.
