## What You Are Actually Buying

Airbnb revenue management is the practice of deciding what each night should sell for, and under what conditions, so that revenue per available night is as high as the market allows. It covers nightly rate, minimum stay, length-of-stay pricing, fees, promotions and pacing against the booking curve. A pricing tool is not the same thing. A tool executes those decisions once somebody has made them.

The discipline came out of airlines, which is where I spent ten years before this. A seat on tomorrow's flight and a night in your calendar share one property: once the date passes, whatever went unsold is worth nothing, permanently. You cannot warehouse it and sell it next week.

<div class="rf-band cedar">
  <p class="kicker">SNIPPET DEFINITION</p>
  <h3>What is an Airbnb revenue management company?</h3>
  <p>An Airbnb revenue management company is a service that manages pricing strategy, calendar availability, minimum-stay rules and length-of-stay decisions on an owner's behalf, without taking over operational control of the property. The owner keeps guests, cleaning and listings; the company owns the revenue discipline. It sits between dynamic pricing software, which automates decisions you make, and full-service property management, which absorbs pricing along with everything else.</p>
</div>

Three categories, and they are not three quality tiers. They are three different products:

- **Software** executes the pricing changes you decide on, automating the mechanics against rules you configure. You remain the strategist.
- **A managed service** decides and executes. You delegate the judgment. A person watches your calendar, reads your market, and makes the calls a rules engine will not make on its own.
- **A full-service property manager** decides, executes, and does everything else: guests, cleaning, maintenance, listings, owner reporting. Revenue management is one of thirty jobs.

Software is not the weaker product here. A pricing engine reprices thousands of listings faster and more consistently than any person, and for a single clean listing in a market its owner understands, that is most of the available value.

## How We Evaluated These Companies

**Scoring:** each vendor gets **0, 1 or 2** on each of the seven levers, for a maximum of **14**.

- **2**: the vendor owns this lever as a decision, on your behalf
- **1**: partial: automated, rules-only, advisory, or diagnosed but not executed
- **0**: out of scope

Scores were read from each vendor's own public product documentation and stated scope, not from testing their service.

## Common Mistakes When Choosing an Airbnb Revenue Management Company

- **Shortlisting a vendor before scoring your own calendar.** The most expensive mistake available, because a managed service cannot fix a photo problem and a tool cannot supply discipline.
- **Counting one vendor twice.** STR Consulting and Pacer on the same shortlist is the current version of this.
- **Reading "dedicated revenue manager" on a software quote as a person.** Check which product line it belongs to and what it costs separately.
- **Assuming a vendor will take you.** Ask the portfolio minimum in the first five minutes. Pacer's floor rules out most single-property owners before anything else is discussed.
- **Comparing a flat fee to a percentage without doing the arithmetic at your own revenue.** At $70,000 a year the gap is roughly $4,200 against $240 to $700. At $400,000 across a portfolio the comparison inverts in places.
- **Treating a high lever score as a recommendation.** Coverage you do not need is just cost.
- **Accepting "we'll discuss pricing on the call" as normal.** It is normal in this category, and it still favours the seller.
