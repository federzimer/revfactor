## How We Scored the Tools

Each tool was scored out of 100 on six criteria, weighted before any tool was scored and applied identically to all nine:

- **Pricing controls (25):** minimum stay and gap-night automation, event and seasonal rules, strategies and the number of settings you can change.
- **Cost for one listing (20):** the published monthly price for a single listing, and whether a flat or percentage option exists.
- **Channel and PMS connections (20):** direct connections to Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com, and the property management systems it works with.
- **Multi-listing management (15):** portfolio views, grouping and bulk rules.
- **Rate transparency (10):** whether the tool shows why it set a rate.
- **Trial and terms (10):** free trial length, whether a card is needed and how easily you can cancel.

Sources are each vendor's own pricing, feature and integration pages, checked on September 16, 2026, plus Airbnb's Help Center article on Smart Pricing. Where a vendor does not publish something, the tool scores lower on that criterion rather than getting the benefit of the doubt. Ties are broken by the pricing controls score.

## 1. PriceLabs

**Best for:** hosts who want the deepest set of pricing controls and the widest channel coverage.

**Score: 92** (controls 24, cost 18, channels 20, multi-listing 14, transparency 6, trial and terms 10)

PriceLabs' dynamic pricing runs automated daily rate and minimum stay updates from market data and your listing's performance. It offers 30+ customizations, base price and minimum stay recommendations, last-minute discounts and far-out pricing, plus listing performance reports and portfolio analytics with unlimited users on one account.

The pricing page shows $14.49 per listing per month by default, but that figure is the rate at ten listings. On the same calculator, one US listing costs $19.99 a month, two cost $17.49 each and three cost $16.66 each. There is also a plan at 1% of booking revenue. The 30-day free trial needs no card, you can pause or cancel anytime, and one daily sync is included, with extra syncs at $1 per listing per month.

PriceLabs connects directly to Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com and integrates with more than 150 property management systems. It loses the most points on rate transparency, because it explains its recommendations less directly than AirDNA Adapt or Hospitable, and smaller amounts on controls, cost and multi-listing tools. RevFactor runs PriceLabs for its clients, which is covered in [the head-to-head against Wheelhouse and a done-for-you service](/blog/pricelabs-vs-wheelhouse-vs-done-for-you/).

## 2. Wheelhouse

**Best for:** owners and managers who want to build and compare pricing strategies across a portfolio.

**Score: 86** (controls 22, cost 18, channels 15, multi-listing 15, transparency 8, trial and terms 8)

Wheelhouse lets you choose between three strategies and 15+ settings, mix rule-based and data-driven pricing, set custom rates for key dates and price up to 18 months ahead. Its portfolio tools are the strongest here: segments, dynamic sets, a portfolio view of strategies and calendars, version history to roll back changes, and notes and alerts for a team.

Pro Flat costs $19.99 per listing per month, falling to $16.99 from 10 to 49 listings. Pro Flex costs 1% of revenue with a $2.99 monthly minimum, billed when a reservation is made and refunded if it cancels. Dynamic Sets cost $12.99 per set per month. Support runs 24/7 by email and chat, with Zoom calls.

Wheelhouse lists a direct Airbnb connection plus property management software and channel managers including Guesty, Hostaway, Hospitable, Hostfully, OwnerRez, Streamline and Uplisting. It scores below PriceLabs on channels because its integrations page does not list direct Vrbo or Booking.com connections.

## 3. DPGO

**Best for:** hosts who want to pay only when the tool produces a booking.

**Score: 78** (controls 20, cost 19, channels 12, multi-listing 11, transparency 6, trial and terms 10)

DPGO describes itself as AI-driven dynamic pricing for hosts, vacation rental owners and short-term rental managers. Each listing has 30+ settings covering price limits, custom rates, discounts, minimum night stays, weekend and seasonal adjustments and predefined strategies, and its analysis includes 20+ competing listings per property and holiday predictions.

Pricing is either a flat $18 per listing per month or $1 per booked night. The 30-day free trial needs no card, there is no setup fee and you can cancel any time. Support runs 24/7 by email, with live chat.

Its partners page names Airbnb and property management software including Hostaway, iGMS and Hostfully. DPGO scores lower on channels and multi-listing tools than the top two because it publishes less detail on either.

## 4. AirDNA Adapt

**Best for:** hosts who want to understand every rate before they let a tool set it.

**Score: 77** (controls 18, cost 18, channels 12, multi-listing 9, transparency 10, trial and terms 10)

Adapt is AirDNA's dynamic pricing product, built on the market data AirDNA already sells. AirDNA says it tracks more than 15 million listings, and Adapt draws on a de-duplicated view of Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com. Instead of hundreds of settings, you choose one of four named strategies, and every rate shows the math behind it. It earns the top score for rate transparency on that basis.

Adapt costs $20 per listing per month with a 30-day free trial, and AirDNA says you can connect for free and see every rate before a trial starts. It connects directly to Airbnb and works with Hostaway, Guesty and Uplisting through Price Sync.

It scores lower on controls and multi-listing tools by design. Four strategies are easier to use than 30 customizations, but they leave less room to tune a listing with unusual demand. AirDNA sells a separate property manager plan for portfolios of six or more properties.

## 5. Hospitable

**Best for:** hosts who want pricing inside the same software that runs their calendars, messages and cleaning.

**Score: 77** (controls 14, cost 15, channels 18, multi-listing 11, transparency 9, trial and terms 10)

Hospitable is property management software with dynamic pricing built in. It adjusts nightly rates based on demand, competition and seasonality, shows how base price, demand, seasonality and lead time add up to each rate, and lets you preview recommended prices before you switch it on.

Dynamic pricing is included on the Host plan, which costs $25.52 a month for one property and allows up to two, with the second property at $10. On the free Essentials plan, dynamic pricing is free for three bookings in your first 30 days and then costs $15 per property. Every plan syncs Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com and Agoda, and the 14-day trial needs no card.

Hospitable loses points on controls because its pricing settings are simpler than a dedicated tool's. It ties with AirDNA Adapt at 77 and ranks fifth on the tiebreak.

## 6. Beyond

**Best for:** hosts who would rather pay a share of bookings than a fixed fee per listing.

**Score: 75** (controls 22, cost 14, channels 12, multi-listing 13, transparency 6, trial and terms 8)

Beyond has sold dynamic pricing to short-term rental owners and managers since 2013. The Growth plan includes minimum stay and gap-fill automation, dynamic occupancy and time-based adjustments, custom demand factor sensitivity, booking curves, pacing reports, hyperlocal neighborhood data and multi-unit mapping. It also describes event detection and forward-looking demand signals.

Growth costs 1% of bookings and you can start free with a $50 credit. Pro costs 1.25% of bookings, adds search-powered pricing, search insights and custom comp sets, and starts with a demo. A percentage plan costs more as revenue grows, which is why Beyond scores lower on cost than the flat-fee tools.

Beyond lists two-way PMS integrations. Its plans page does not name direct channel connections, so it scores lower on channels here.

## 7. Quibble

**Best for:** portfolios that want pricing built on genuinely comparable units rather than a regional average.

**Score: 73** (controls 22, cost 6, channels 14, multi-listing 15, transparency 9, trial and terms 7)

Quibble's RevenueOS prices each night against like-kind comparable units, grades that comp set and shows you every unit in it, so you can see why a rate moved. It reads how those comparable units price into events and holidays, and it manages the whole portfolio from one calendar with a daily insights brief.

The subscription is a flat fee per listing that depends on portfolio size, never a commission. Quibble shows your exact price through a quote builder that asks for your email, so it scores low on published cost. Service is month to month with no setup fees. It works with Guesty, Hostaway, Hostfully, OwnerRez, Lodgify, 365Villas, Escapia, Hospitable and Streamline, and Quibble also sells Revenue Desk, a managed service, for portfolios of 20+ listings.

## 8. Revenuenaire Dynamic Pricing

**Best for:** hosts who want a low flat price and the option of paying someone to set it up.

**Score: 71** (controls 16, cost 19, channels 12, multi-listing 10, transparency 6, trial and terms 8)

Revenuenaire, which also sells managed revenue management, offers its own dynamic pricing software. It sets market-driven base rates and rules, shows competitor rates per date and pushes rates to your channels automatically.

It costs $17.99 a month for one listing, $15.49 per listing at two and $12.49 per listing at ten, billed per active listing with no seats and no setup fees. You can import listings for free and pay when you activate. An optional expert setup, from $125 once, has a Revenuenaire revenue manager configure base rates, seasonality, minimum stays and gap-filling for you.

It scores lower on controls, channels and transparency because its site publishes less detail on each than the tools above it.

## 9. Airbnb Smart Pricing

**Best for:** brand-new hosts who list only on Airbnb and want a free starting point.

**Score: 47** (controls 6, cost 20, channels 3, multi-listing 5, transparency 3, trial and terms 10)

Smart Pricing is built into Airbnb and costs nothing. Airbnb says it uses hundreds of factors about your listing and your area to adjust your nightly price based on demand, within a minimum and maximum you set. You can override any night with a custom price and switch it off at any time.

The limits are in Airbnb's own Help Center. Weekly, monthly and trip-length discounts override Smart Pricing, you have to turn it off to add weekend pricing, and it overrides saved rule-sets. It only prices Airbnb and does not explain individual rates, which is why it scores last.

## Which Pricing Tool Should You Choose?

**How many listings do you run?** For one or two, the gap between PriceLabs, Wheelhouse, DPGO, AirDNA Adapt and Revenuenaire is about $2 a month, so choose on controls and transparency. Past ten listings, per-listing discounts at PriceLabs, Wheelhouse and Revenuenaire start to matter.

**Which channels do you list on?** If you list on Vrbo or Booking.com as well as Airbnb and do not use a PMS, PriceLabs and Hospitable cover all three directly. If you run a PMS, check that the tool integrates with yours.

**Flat fee or percentage?** A percentage plan (Beyond, or the 1% options at PriceLabs and Wheelhouse) costs little in a slow month and more in a strong one. Compare both at your expected annual bookings.

**Will you actually open it every week?** A tool sets rates from the settings you give it. If nobody reviews those settings as the market moves, a better tool will not fix that. Our guide on [whether a pricing tool is enough on its own](/blog/is-a-pricing-tool-enough-airbnb-revenue-management/) walks through that test, and the [beginner's guide to dynamic pricing](/blog/dynamic-pricing-str-beginners-guide/) covers how these tools work.
