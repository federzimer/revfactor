## The script

### 0:00 to 0:15 · Cold open (hook)
> "I spent ten years selling airline seats before I ever managed a single Airbnb. And the day I started managing rentals, I realized almost every host is leaving money on the table in a way an airline never would. Here is the whole system, in three parts."

**On screen:** "The 3-Part System" (Cormorant, cedar on bone).

### 0:15 to 0:55 · The premise
> "An airline sells the same seat at forty different prices, and by the time that plane takes off, they have squeezed every dollar out of it. A host does the opposite. They pick one price, maybe turn on a pricing tool, and leave it. The problem is not the tool. It is that nobody is thinking like a revenue manager. So let me show you how I do."

### 0:55 to 2:25 · Part 1: Inventory awareness
> "Part one. Every night in your calendar is a seat on a plane. It is perishable. If tonight goes unbooked, that revenue is gone forever, you cannot sell tonight tomorrow. Most hosts protect their nightly rate like it is precious and let whole nights expire empty. An airline would never do that. So the first shift is simple. Stop thinking about the price of a night and start thinking about the cost of an empty one. That single change is what makes you willing to flex your minimum-stay, open up orphan gaps, and actually fill the calendar."

**On screen:** a calendar where empty nights fade out like departed flights. Caption: "An empty night is a departed seat."

### 2:25 to 3:55 · Part 2: Price to demand, not to a number
> "Part two. Airlines do not pick a price. They read demand and let the price find its level. Fare classes fill up, and the price climbs as the plane fills. You should run your calendar the same way. Do not set your Fourth of July rate in January and walk away. Start a little under the market so you build booking momentum, watch how fast the dates fill, and raise the rate into that demand. You are not guessing. The market is telling you how high it will go, and you are going there in steps."

**On screen:** a fare-class style graphic, price climbing as seats/nights fill.

### 3:55 to 5:25 · Part 3: Pace against the market, not last year
> "Part three, and this is the one hosts miss the most. They look at what they made last year and copy it. That is a photograph. You need the movie. Every week, an airline compares how fast this flight is booking to the same flight last year, and they adjust. You do the same with your calendar. Booking faster than last year? Demand is higher than you thought, so the price goes up. Booking slower? Do not panic and slash the rate. Adjust the minimum-stay first and protect your price. That weekly rhythm is the entire difference between hoping and managing."

**On screen:** two pacing curves, this year vs last year, with weekly adjustment points.

### 5:25 to end · Wrap + CTA
> "Inventory awareness, price to demand, pace against the market. That is how an airline thinks, and that is how we run every calendar at RevFactor. If you would rather have this done for you than do it every week yourself, the link is in the description. Book a strategy call and we will look at your calendar together."

**On screen:** "Inventory. Demand. Pace. | RevFactor · revfactor.io"
