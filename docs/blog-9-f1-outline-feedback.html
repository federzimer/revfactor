Must-fix before draft (3 items)

Fix 1 — URL slug missing the /blog/ prefix

Brief proposes /f1-race-weekend-str-revenue-playbook. The Astro content collection routes all posts under /blog/, and the brief's slug doesn't include it. Same trap GetCito's Blog 7 hit — when shipped, the post landed at /blog/blog/... until the slug was stripped from frontmatter.

Fix: drop the slug: field entirely from frontmatter. Astro routes filename-based, so:

src/content/blog/f1-race-weekend-str-pricing-playbook.mdx

resolves automatically to /blog/f1-race-weekend-str-pricing-playbook/. Recommended filename change: drop "revenue" → "pricing" to match the FIFA sister post pattern (fifa-2026-world-cup-str-pricing).
