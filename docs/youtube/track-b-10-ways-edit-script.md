## 6. Accuracy flags to clear before this ships

1. **The Airbnb fee-structure reel (`DNzPFjBWPVf`, Aug 2025) is deliberately excluded.** It
   describes marking rates up 15.5% to absorb the host-pays-all fee, and at the time it was
   a rollout being tested on PMS-connected US listings. Correct advice then, unverified for
   a video titled 2026. If we want it as an eleventh way, Fede re-confirms the current fee
   split first.
2. **The Sep 2023 reel about where pricing controls live in the Airbnb UI (`CxIzAQbuCaE`)
   is excluded.** The UI has moved since. A walkthrough of a stale menu dates the whole
   video.
3. **One transcript in the corpus is not Fede.** `20231203_C0Zv3y8vrnl` is a collaborator's
   promo for a joint pricing webinar, first person, different presenter. It is the only one
   I found, but it means the brain corpus is not a guaranteed-Fede set and clip selection
   cannot be automated on transcript text alone. Excluded here.
4. **Two reels from 2026-06-05 (`DKiNMtPPFcQ`, `DKhvBcAxnKl`, duplicate transcripts of the
   same announcement) offer a "free audit."** `AGENTS.md` states RevFactor does not offer a
   free audit and the CTA is a strategy call. Excluded, and worth knowing the claim exists in
   public reels.
