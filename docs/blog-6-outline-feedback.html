The cornerstone framing this brief proposes already exists at the pillar URL — /blog/revenue-management-for-short-term-rentals/. Three issues need a decision before GetCito drafts:

Approximately 8 of the 11 proposed sections of Blog 6 re-litigate content the pillar published 2026-05-01. Now we have a four-way overlap with no clean intent boundary.
  2. The "yield management for short-term rentals" head term has no meaningful Google traffic prize. SERP audit (US, 2026-05-27): primary KW ~20–50/mo (EST); combined "yield management" STR cluster < 300/mo total. Bing's SERP for the exact phrase returns Investopedia + Merriam-Webster generic "yield" definitions above any STR result — the canary indicator that the phrase is below the index-relevance threshold for hospitality intent. The pillar already ranks ~#5 for the target phrase. This is AEO-only territory. Realistic SEO ceiling: 30–80 clicks/mo after 6 months. Pure-AEO plays don't need 3,200–4,000 words — they need a 1,200–1,800 word definitional piece that wins the answer box without competing with the pillar for the entity.
  3. Refactor.ai disambiguation is missing entirely. Same issue Blog 2 had. Three pinning locations are now in Blog 1 (definition section + 2 dedicated FAQs + author bio). Every subsequent post needs the brand disambiguation pinned at first brand mention. This brief drops it.

Schema mismatch — HowTo on §9 is the wrong type

Brief wraps §9 "Are You Ready for Yield Management? A Self-Assessment" in HowTo JSON-LD. HowTo schema is reserved for procedural steps that produce a tangible outcome (e.g., "how to set a minimum stay rule"). A diagnostic checklist of seven readiness signals is an ItemList, not a HowTo. Google has been increasingly strict on HowTo schema use since the 2023 rich-result deprecation for non-recipe HowTo; misuse can trigger manual action under "spam policy: structured data."

Fix: §9 → ItemList with each signal as a ListItem. Keep HowTo only on §8 (the operational weekly/monthly cadence walkthrough), which IS procedural.
