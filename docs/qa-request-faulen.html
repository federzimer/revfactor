What we would like you to check

No technical knowledge needed. All of this is reading the pages the way a customer would.

  • Is anything factually wrong? Especially a company name, a price, or a claim about what a company actually does.
  • Does it read like a person wrote it? Flag anything stiff, repetitive or robotic.
  • Do the source links work, and do they say what we claim they say? Click a fair few. A link that opens is not the same as a link that backs up the sentence it is attached to.
  • Does it look as good as our best articles? Charts, images and tables should be spread throughout, not a wall of text.
  • No names, anywhere. Never a client, owner or property name. Results are described by market and property type only, for example "a Gatlinburg cabin".
  • Anything that makes you wince. If a claim feels too strong or a comparison feels unfair, say so, even if you cannot say exactly why.
