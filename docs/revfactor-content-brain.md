## 5. The Two Proprietary Frameworks

### Framework 1 — Strategic Philosophy: Interest + Reliability + Positioning
*(Derived from airline yield management.)*

| Pillar | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Interest | Conversion optimization. Listing photos, title, descriptions, amenity completeness |
| Reliability | Review velocity and quality. Pricing that attracts the right guests. Algorithm trust |
| Positioning | Daily pricing relative to the competitive set, using pacing data and RevPAR |

### Framework 2 — Operational Methodology: The Four Pillars of Revenue Management
*(How RevFactor executes daily on each property.)*

1. **Historical Data** — understand 1–2 years of past market performance
2. **Inventory Management** — strategic minimum stays, calendar blackouts, length-of-stay rules
3. **Forecasting** — predict future demand
4. **Pricing Strategy** — dynamic pricing + market penetration + gap-filling + length-of-stay discounts

### How to position the two frameworks
- Framework 1 (Three Pillars) is Federico's **signature** — lead with this.
- Framework 2 (Four Pillars) sits **underneath** — operational discipline.

> "A lot of hosts use what every other host in the market is using as a minimum stay. For example, a lake house in Michigan, everybody around me is doing seven nights minimum, I'll do the same. ## 8. Tactical Plays — Federico's Documented Playbook

### 1. Long Weekend Strategy ("Fin de Largo")
- **3 nights:** 10% off
- **4 nights:** 20% off weekends

### 2. Gap Filling
Apply 20% discount on Tue/Wed only when booked as part of a longer stay. Boosts Airbnb search visibility via strikethrough pricing.

### 3. Length-of-Stay Discount Ladder
Airline-style tiered pricing.

### 4. Minimum Stay as a Competitive Weapon
Fewer restrictions = more search visibility = premium bookings. Newport Beach example: 2-night minimum in a 3-night market captured a 4-night premium booking.

### 5. New Listing Launch Pricing — Market Penetration Strategy
Federico's exact playbook:
1. Close the calendar to two months out
2. Offer the 20% Airbnb new-listing discount
3. Burn the discount fast on early stays, not future ones
4. Compromise initial revenue for the ramp-up
5. Once reviews accumulate, the property graduates from Airbnb's "trial period"

### 6. PMS-Aligned Strategy
Set up the pricing tool to read directly from the PMS so calendar gaps trigger automatic LOS adjustments.
